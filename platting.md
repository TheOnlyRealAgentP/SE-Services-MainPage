faqs:
  - q: Isn't a plat just a survey?
    a: No. A survey measures what's on the ground. A plat is the legal document that sets how the property can be developed, including lot lines, utility easements and building setbacks. It's built on a survey but has to be approved by a city, county or MUD.
  - q: Does my property need to be platted?
    a: If you're subdividing, or the property has never been platted and you need a building permit, it probably does. The rules depend on whether the property is inside a city, in a city's extraterritorial jurisdiction (ETJ), in a MUD or in unincorporated county.
