faqs:
  - q: Is a plat the same as a survey?
    a: No. A survey measures existing conditions on the property. A plat is the legal document that establishes how the property can be developed, including lot lines, utility easements and building setbacks. It is based on a survey but must be approved by a city, county or MUD.
  - q: Does my property need to be platted?
    a: If you are subdividing, or if the property has never been platted and you need a building permit, a plat is likely required. The requirements depend on whether the property is within city limits, a city's extraterritorial jurisdiction (ETJ), a MUD or unincorporated county.
