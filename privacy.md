## What we collect

When you fill out the request form, we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email address (if you give one), town, the service you need and your message. When you call or email us, we see your phone number or email address.

We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies on this website.

## How we use it

We use your information to answer your request, schedule work, send quotes and invoices, and keep the records our licenses and permits require (for example, maintenance contracts and inspection reports filed with the county).

## Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only when we need to:

- **When the law requires it.**
