faqs:
  - q: My alarm is on. What should I do?
    a: First, check the breaker for the septic system. If it has tripped, reset it once. If it trips again, or if it had not tripped, call our office and limit water use, such as laundry, dishwashing and long showers, until the problem is found.
  - q: Why are my sprinklers barely spraying?
    a: The sprinkler pump runs only when the pump tank fills, so weak spray during operation usually indicates a broken sprinkler line, clogged heads, or a pump that is running dry or wearing out.
  - q: What causes an odor in the yard?
    a: A properly functioning aerobic system should produce very little odor. A strong odor usually means the air compressor has stopped, the disinfection unit is out of chlorine, or the tanks need to be pumped.
  - q: How often should the tanks be pumped?
    a: We generally recommend pumping every two to three years. Higher water use, such as frequent guests, heavy laundry loads or additional occupants, may require more frequent pumping.
