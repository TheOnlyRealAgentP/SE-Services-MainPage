  - q: What's the difference between a SWPPP and a SWQMP?
    a: A SWPPP (Storm Water Pollution Prevention Plan) covers construction, meaning how you keep mud and pollutants out of storm water while the site is being built. A SWQMP (Storm Water Quality Management Plan) covers the finished site and how its storm water stays clean over the long term.
