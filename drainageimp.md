  - q: What is the difference between a SWPPP and a SWQMP?
    a: A SWPPP (Storm Water Pollution Prevention Plan) applies during construction and describes how sediment and pollutants are kept out of storm water while the site is being built. A SWQMP (Storm Water Quality Management Plan) applies to the completed site and describes how its storm water quality is maintained over the long term.

    a: In many cases, yes. Building in a floodplain generally requires a floodplain analysis and mitigation measures such as fill limits, elevated floor levels or additional storage. We perform the analysis and design the mitigation.
