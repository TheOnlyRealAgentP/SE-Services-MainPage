Channelview

Harris County · our home office

Our office is on Shady Lane, so Channelview is home. It's an unincorporated community on the San Jacinto River and the Houston Ship Channel, which means county rules, not city rules, cover most septic, platting and drainage work here. Many homes on larger lots rely on aerobic systems, and local businesses come to us for site plans, plats and fire code help.

Popular here: maintenance contracts · repairs · platting · request a quote in Channelview

Baytown

Harris and Chambers counties

Baytown sits where the San Jacinto River and the Houston Ship Channel meet Galveston Bay, and the city crosses the line between Harris and Chambers counties, so which county's rules apply depends on the address. Homes on acreage around the edges of the city often use aerobic systems, and commercial growth along I-10 and SH 146 means a steady need for drainage, detention and platting.

Popular here: installation · drainage & detention · land development · request a quote in Baytown

Crosby

Harris County

Crosby is an unincorporated community in northeast Harris County along US 90 and FM 2100. Many homes here sit on acreage with aerobic systems that need contracts, inspections and the occasional repair. One of our HomeAdvisor reviews comes from a Crosby customer who liked that we can maintain, repair and pump a system.

Popular here: maintenance contracts · repairs & pumping · installation · request a quote in Crosby

Highlands

Harris County

Highlands is an unincorporated community on the east side of the San Jacinto River, a short drive from our office. Many properties are on septic, and parts of the area sit close to the river and its floodplain. That affects where a new system can go and what a new building or addition needs before it's permitted.

Popular here: installation · maintenance contracts · floodplain analysis · request a quote in Highlands

Huffman

Harris County

Huffman is in far northeast Harris County on the east side of Lake Houston. Much of it is rural acreage and lake-area lots without city sewer, so aerobic systems are common, and a system has to be designed around each lot's soil and space.

Popular here: installation · maintenance contracts · wastewater design · request a quote in Huffman

Mont Belvieu

Chambers County

Mont Belvieu is a fast-growing city east of Baytown along I-10, in Chambers County rather than Harris County, so permits and plats go through different offices than they do in Channelview. New subdivisions and commercial sites need platting, drainage and utility planning, and homes outside sewer service rely on septic.

Popular here: platting · land development · installation · request a quote in Mont Belvieu

Deer Park

Harris County

Deer Park is a city on the Houston Ship Channel and home to several large refineries and chemical plants. Most homes are on city sewer, so the services that matter most here are engineering ones: fire code and operational permits, fire protection, and site drainage for commercial and industrial properties.

Popular here: fire code & operational permits · fire protection · drainage · request a quote in Deer Park

La Porte

Harris County

La Porte sits on Galveston Bay near the Barbours Cut and Bayport container terminals. Warehouses, industrial sites and commercial buildings tied to the port need site plans, detention and fire code reviews, and properties outside the city's sewer system still need septic service.

Popular here: land development · detention · fire code · request a quote in La Porte

Pasadena

Harris County

Pasadena is one of the largest cities in Harris County, stretching along the south side of the Houston Ship Channel. Its neighborhoods are mostly on city sewer and it has a large industrial base, so most projects here call for engineering: drainage, platting and fire code compliance for commercial sites.

Popular here: platting · drainage · fire code · request a quote in Pasadena
