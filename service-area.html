Channelview

Harris County · our home office

Channelview is home to our office on Shady Lane. As an unincorporated community on the San Jacinto River and the Houston Ship Channel, most septic, platting and drainage work here falls under county regulations rather than city ordinances. Many homes on larger lots rely on aerobic systems, and local businesses regularly require site plans, plats and fire code reviews.

Common services: maintenance contracts · repairs · platting · request a quote in Channelview

Baytown

Harris and Chambers counties

Baytown sits where the San Jacinto River and the Houston Ship Channel meet Galveston Bay, and the city crosses the line between Harris and Chambers counties, so the applicable county regulations depend on the address. Homes on acreage around the edges of the city often use aerobic systems, and commercial growth along I-10 and SH 146 creates ongoing demand for drainage, detention and platting services.

Common services: installation · drainage & detention · land development · request a quote in Baytown

Crosby

Harris County

Crosby is an unincorporated community in northeast Harris County along US 90 and FM 2100. Many homes here sit on acreage with aerobic systems that require maintenance contracts, regular inspections and periodic repairs. One of our HomeAdvisor reviews comes from a Crosby customer who valued our ability to maintain, repair and pump their system.

Common services: maintenance contracts · repairs & pumping · installation · request a quote in Crosby

Highlands

Harris County

Highlands is an unincorporated community on the east side of the San Jacinto River, a short drive from our office. Many properties are served by septic systems, and parts of the area lie close to the river and its floodplain. This can affect where a new system may be located and what a new building or addition requires before it can be permitted.

Common services: installation · maintenance contracts · floodplain analysis · request a quote in Highlands

Huffman

Harris County

Huffman is in far northeast Harris County on the east side of Lake Houston. Much of it is rural acreage and lake-area lots without city sewer, so aerobic systems are common and each system must be designed for the lot's soil and available space.

Common services: installation · maintenance contracts · wastewater design · request a quote in Huffman

Mont Belvieu

Chambers County

Mont Belvieu is a fast-growing city east of Baytown along I-10, in Chambers County rather than Harris County, so permits and plats are handled by different offices than in Channelview. New subdivisions and commercial sites require platting, drainage and utility planning, and homes outside the sewer service area rely on septic systems.

Common services: platting · land development · installation · request a quote in Mont Belvieu

Deer Park

Harris County

Deer Park is a city on the Houston Ship Channel and home to several large refineries and chemical plants. Most homes are on city sewer, so our engineering services are most relevant here: fire code compliance and operational permits, fire protection, and site drainage for commercial and industrial properties.

Common services: fire code & operational permits · fire protection · drainage · request a quote in Deer Park

La Porte

Harris County

La Porte sits on Galveston Bay near the Barbours Cut and Bayport container terminals. Warehouses, industrial sites and commercial buildings associated with the port require site plans, detention and fire code reviews, and properties outside the city's sewer system require septic service.

Common services: land development · detention · fire code · request a quote in La Porte

Pasadena

Harris County

Pasadena is one of the largest cities in Harris County, stretching along the south side of the Houston Ship Channel. Its neighborhoods are largely on city sewer and it has a large industrial base, so most projects here involve engineering services: drainage, platting and fire code compliance for commercial sites.

Common services: platting · drainage · fire code · request a quote in Pasadena
