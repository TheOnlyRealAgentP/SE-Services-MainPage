faqs:
  - q: What is the difference between land development and platting?
    a: Platting is one step in the process. It legally establishes lots, easements and building lines. Land development encompasses the complete plan for the property, including layout, utilities, drainage and approvals. Most projects require both, and we provide both.
  - q: Do I need a survey before starting?
    a: In most cases, yes. A boundary survey, and often a topographic survey, is the starting point for design. If you do not have one, we will advise you on the type of survey required.
