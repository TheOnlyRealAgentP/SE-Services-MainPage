faqs:
  - q: What's the difference between land development and platting?
    a: Platting is one step. It legally defines the lots, easements and building lines. Land development is the whole plan for the property, covering how it's laid out, served by utilities, drained and approved. Most projects need both, and we handle both.
  - q: Do I need a survey first?
    a: Usually, yes. A boundary survey, and often a topographic survey, is the starting point for design. If you don't have one, we'll tell you what kind you need.
