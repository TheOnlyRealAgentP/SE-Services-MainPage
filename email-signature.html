Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on a computer, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  3. Paste the signature into the box, then click Save changes at the bottom of the page.

Outlook

  1. Outlook on the web: Settings (gear) → Mail → Compose and reply. Outlook for Windows: File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  2. Create a new signature, paste, and save.
