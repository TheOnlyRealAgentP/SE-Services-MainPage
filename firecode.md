faqs:
  - q: Which fire code applies to my building?
    a: Most Texas jurisdictions adopt an edition of the International Fire Code with local amendments. Harris County and each city adopt their own edition, so the applicable code depends on the building's location.
    todo: verify adopted fire code editions for Harris County and cities served
  - q: What is an operational permit?
    a: An operational permit is authorization from the fire marshal, in unincorporated Harris County the Harris County Fire Marshal's Office (FMO), for certain activities or uses, such as storing hazardous materials or operating a place of assembly. The permits you need depend on how the building is used.
    todo: verify Harris County Fire Marshal's Office operational permit categories
  - q: Do I need an engineer for fire code compliance?
    a: Not always. Some items can be addressed directly by the owner. When the fire marshal requires engineered plans, calculations or a sealed letter, they must be prepared by a licensed engineer.
